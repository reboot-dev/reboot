PASS if every list of events, history or past items the design records sits on the short-lived entity it explains (one kudos, one order), in an OrderedMap, or on a per-period actor (a day, a month), with a reason such as "grows without bound" or "every write re-serializes it"; a design that records no history list also passes. FAIL if a list that grows with use (every kudos ever given, every event) is a plain list field on the board, the team or another actor that lives as long as the app. Quote what you relied on.
